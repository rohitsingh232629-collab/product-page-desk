What we find

The small mistakes that quietly cost you orders

These are real things we found on small Indian stores in November 2026. We've left the names out. Most founders never see them, because they read their own page on a laptop and already know the product.

!

Ingredients that don't match

A list that leaves out an ingredient named in the product's own title, which matters to anyone with an allergy or a diet.

⇄

Copy pasted from another product

A grain's page describing a different grain in several places, or a "sugar-free" bar with an FAQ about sugar.

§

Wording worth a second look

Lines that read like medical claims on a snack. We flag them so you can check them with your compliance adviser.

↓

Add to Cart buried

On a phone, the buy button can sit two or more screens down, below the gallery and a long description.

◷

Out-of-date dates

Festive dispatch windows or "this summer" notes still showing after the date has passed.

G

Broken Google titles

Titles cut off mid-word, typos and raw code in what shoppers see on Google, plus images with no alt text.

An AI tool can rewrite text you paste into it. It won't tell you that your page contradicts your label. That takes reading your page closely, which is what we do.
