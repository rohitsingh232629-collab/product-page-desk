What we find

These are real examples from small Indian stores we read in November 2026, with the names left out:

  • An ingredients list that left out a key ingredient named in the product's own title.
  • A grain's page describing a different grain, in several places.
  • A snack page with wording that reads like a medical claim.
  • Add to Cart buried more than two phone screens down.
  • Festive dispatch dates still showing after the window had passed.

An AI tool can rewrite text you paste into it. It won't tell you that your page contradicts your label. That takes reading your page closely, which is the part most founders don't have time for.
